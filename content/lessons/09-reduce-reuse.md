## Why the order matters

The phrase is **"Reduce, Reuse, Recycle"** — *in that order*. Recycling is the **last** resort, not the first.

### Reduce (best)

The single most effective action is to consume less of what becomes ocean plastic in the first place:

- Choose a refillable water bottle.
- Choose loose fruit and vegetables instead of plastic-wrapped.
- Skip single-use cutlery; carry a small reusable set.
- Buy products with minimal packaging.
- Push your school / cafeteria to remove disposable plates.

### Reuse (great)

- Glass jars become storage containers.
- Plastic bottles become plant pots, bird feeders, art.
- T-shirts become rags.
- Use refill stations for shampoo and detergent.

### Recycle (last resort)

- Sort. Clean. Dry. Place in the correct bin.
- Even when recycling works, only ~9% of all plastic ever produced has been recycled. Don't trust recycling to fix the problem alone.

## A 7-day teen challenge

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Mon | Bring a refillable bottle to school. |
| Tue | Refuse a plastic straw at a café. |
| Wed | Bring lunch in a reusable container. |
| Thu | Donate or repurpose one unused item at home. |
| Fri | Skip plastic packaging at the grocery store. |
| Sat | Talk to a friend about TideGuard. |
| Sun | Submit one new TideGuard report or join a cleanup. |


```task
- 30-minute mini-task: pick one street, river or beach near you and walk it. Photograph any plastic item bigger than a coin, then log it via the TideGuard app.
- Goal: at least 5 reports with photo + GPS within 30 minutes.
- Wrap-up: write 2 sentences in your notebook describing what surprised you.
```
